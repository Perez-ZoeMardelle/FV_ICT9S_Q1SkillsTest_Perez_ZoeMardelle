Welcome, Future Roboticist!

The OBMC Robotics Club is a community of students who love building, programming, and competing with robots. Whether you're a total beginner or already know your way around a microcontroller, this club is your space to learn hands-on skills, work in teams, and represent our school in robotics competitions. Fill out the application form below to become part of our next batch of members. We can't wait to build the future with you!

Steps to Join

  1. Read the club overview and requirements.
  2. Fill out the application form below.
  3. Wait for confirmation from the club moderator.

What You'll Need

  • Enthusiasm to learn something new
  • A notebook for documenting builds and code
  • Willingness to work in a team

Key Terms

Microcontroller
A small computer (like an Arduino) used to control robot behavior.
Sensor
A component that lets a robot detect things like light, distance, or touch.
Actuator
A part (like a motor) that allows a robot to move or perform actions.
